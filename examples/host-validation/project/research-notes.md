# Research notes (synthetic)

Every name and figure here is invented for the host validation scenario.

## Interviews, October 2026: 20 customers of the fictional portal

- 14 of 20 forward a support request to a colleague at least once a month, by pasting its text into an email.
- 9 of 20 said the colleague gave up because they had no portal account.
- What they ask for: a link the colleague opens without an account.

## Decisions the product owner already took

- The Friday demo runs on synthetic requests only.
- A shared link shows the request's id, status and next step, and the owner's email address (asked by Sales).
- The link never expires, so that a colleague can come back to it.
- Success measure: share of forwarded requests opened by the colleague within 7 days. Baseline: 0, the feature does not exist.
