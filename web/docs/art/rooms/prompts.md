# Prompts des décors d'en-tête

## Organisation

Original premium hand-drawn PIXEL ART environment atlas for Open Guild Kit, an independent RPG-style software product. Crisp 16-bit pixel clusters, strong architecture and readable objects at thumbnail size. Output one extra-wide 2.65:1 horizontal image. EXACTLY four individual panoramic environment illustrations in a precise 2 by 2 grid, each cell one equal quadrant: vertical separator exactly at the center and horizontal separator exactly at the center. Use 8 pixel thin pure black straight separators. NO additional empty margins, NO labels, NO text, NO UI, NO logos, NO framing outside the grid. The four environments belong to the SAME refined mountain guild built out of near-black obsidian slate, dark green-gray stone, aged silver and cool ivory, illuminated by mint lanterns and moonlight. Strict OGK logo palette: black #080d11, slate #142225, #25413e, #4c7169, mint #73f0c2 and ivory #f4f6f3 in highlights; allowed natural intermediate shades only on that green-neutral range. No brown, purple, blue, orange or gold, no high-saturation color except touches of mint, no sepia. Dramatic contrast in a beautiful space, visible midtones and a few bright ivory-and-mint accents. Fine pixel-painted joinery and texture, stepped light and shadows, felted depth, no blurry glow, no gradients over contours. All four views are a low, frontal three-quarter view, not a bird's eye view. Each individual cell has a dark, less detailed left third for a fade overlay, identifying objects located at horizontal 62–84% within the cell, vertically centered. The main silhouette and highlights must remain legible in the center half of the cell's height because the app crops top and bottom. Draw each room only inside its own quadrant. The first look should evoke actual places to explore.
TOP LEFT: kingdom’s arrival gate, great black-granite arches with an ivory portcullis, slender mint lanterns and steps overlooking the near-silent fir town; anchor the pale portal in the right half. TOP RIGHT: blueprint hall, a large angled drafting table with bright ivory roll plan and mint geometry, oversized open dividers and weathered dark stone behind. BOTTOM LEFT: guild atelier alcove, a rich series of handcrafted silver-ivory desk fronts and a lamp illuminating an arc window, clear station and tools but no people. BOTTOM RIGHT: council antechamber, a moonlit pointed vault over an empty pale round arbitration table with papers and three tall chairs, distinct in shape from the drafting room.
