# Instructions de génération du casting

Les cinq planches partagent une directive commune et une description distincte des quatre personnages.

## Directive commune

```text
Production sprite atlas for the original fictional Open Guild Kit RPG. Actual crisp pixel art, 2D 32-bit handheld RPG graphics, deliberate big clusters with pixel-stepped edges, carefully placed little squares of highlights, restrained dithering in shadows and an elegant slight three-quarter front view. Chibi scale, each character is about 2.5 heads tall and full body, their face should be large and expressive with distinct hairstyle, eyebrow and jaw. Same front-left light and identical camera, head size and dense detail across all four. Strong pose and outline, read well on a dark slate background. Restrict colors strictly to the OGK logo family: graphite near-black #020508, charcoal #141d22, dark pine #163831, mid jade #286356, muted slate #7c918d, mint #6ef7c4, pale mint #b5f9e4, paper-white #fdfdfd. Skin tones are diverse shades of quiet neutral grey with slight mint bounced light. No brown, purple, blue, orange, pink, yellow. The surrounding canvas is real alpha transparency, no platform, floor, drop shadow onto the canvas, text, labels, icons outside the silhouettes, or frame. Four detailed isolated full standing characters in exactly one horizontal row in four equal-width columns. Their centers lie at x=12.5%,37.5%,62.5%,87.5% and their feet share one baseline. Full headgear, held props, fingers, footwear and hems visible. Allow generous transparent gutters between columns. Give each sprite compact spacing and clear silhouette; short handheld items never extend into another column. Do not render duplicates or extra characters. Wide landscape sheet. These sprites will be extracted into 80×112 transparent WebP images.
```
