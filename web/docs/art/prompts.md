## Entrée

Original pixel-art environment panorama for the Open Guild Kit application, a nocturnal guild town nestled among firs and a river. Create a single wide 3:2 illustration which will also crop to a wide horizontal band. Precision handmade 16-bit pixel art, defined square clusters, no blurry interpolation, restrained exact palette: near-black teal graphite #0b1417, deep blue spruce #10272b, slate teal #245058, mid teal #33726c, mint highlights #73f0c2, soft warm lanterns #edaf69, muted limestone #c8c6a3. Three-quarters overhead RPG perspective; foreground bridge and still water with tiny pixel reflections, a large original angular slate-and-copper guildhall and its illuminated windows right of center, small outbuildings, layered conifers and craggy mountains behind, quietly lit from upper left. Leave leftmost quarter subordinate, darker forest silhouetted for a UI text overlay, with no features vital at top and bottom. Contrasting light focal structure and quieter far planes. Fine craft: roofs with stepped slate courses, terraced sandstone paths, asymmetrical pine silhouettes, few amber windows. Original world; no imitation of an existing game or visual source. No UI, no text, no letters, no logo, no border, no watermark, no characters. The supplied palette and pixel-square logo aesthetic inform the atmosphere only.

## Guilde

Create one original lavish 16-bit pixel art environment to be used as interactive scene in the Open Guild Kit application. Wide landscape composition, about 2.3:1 if possible, building a unique mountain guild's atelier. High-end handcrafted pixel clusters, stepped crisp contours, clean depth and quiet silhouettes. Elevated three-quarter front-facing view of a richly furnished communal workspace after sunset. The upper third is a huge row of sweeping mullioned windows with terraced slate rooftops of the guild town, firs, distant teal-purple mountains, subdued violet sunset and apricot edge lighting. Architecture in kiln-fired brick, walnut, brass, dark teal slate, copper pendant lamps. In the lower half, design exactly four distinct side-by-side workstation zones spaced evenly from left to right (center x around 14%, 38%, 62%, 86%). Left station a walnut drafting desk with opened plans and brass tools. Inner left a green-felt secretary's desk with inks and a crate of folders. Inner right a smaller amber-lit lantern and wall-mounted scroll rack above a long workbench. Right a librarian's sorting desk under a ladder bookshelf. Keep the floor directly in front of each workstation clear; the app will overlay little original guild sprites with labels there. Characters, signage and UI are excluded from the asset. Add chair shadows, scrap paper, rolled diagrams, plants, bowls, shelf objects and woven rugs for coherent storytelling. Foreground edge is quiet and dark; color palette walnut #63433a, deep purple graphite #171820, warm brass #c58f59 and warm cream #ead5ac, slate #263e46, touches of mint #73f0c2 glass, pink amber city horizon. Strong localized warm lamp light with dark oak in the shadows, restrained detail so the scene reads as one real space. Pixel-art game environment still only, no photorealism, no smooth gradients, no text, no logos, no frame, no characters, no Japanese iconography, no imitation of a specific existing game or app.
